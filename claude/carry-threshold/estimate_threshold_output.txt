==============================================================================
WHERE THE THRESHOLD COMES FROM
==============================================================================
   Nobody publishes the cost of flying an ounce of gold across the
   Atlantic, having it recast on the way. It is estimated from the
   flows: metal does not move until the premium covers that cost, so
   the premium-tonnage relationship kinks, and the kink is the cost.

   139 months, 2015-01 to 2026-07
   premium  $-6.50 to $17.59 an ounce
   tonnes   0.3 to 195.4

==============================================================================
THE RIGHT AGGREGATION: HINGE DAILY, THEN AVERAGE
==============================================================================
   Everything above regresses tonnes on the MONTHLY MEAN premium, which
   is what the earlier estimate did. It is the wrong way round. The
   hinge is convex, so a month averaging zero can contain a week at $5
   that moved metal, and averaging first hides it. The premium should be
   hinged daily and the result averaged:

       tonnes = a + beta * mean_over_days[ max(0, premium - kappa) ]

   kappa is then chosen by profile least squares over the same grid.

   threshold      $+0.78 an ounce
   90% CI         block-6 [$-0.46, $+2.43]
   slope          +8.92 t per $/oz of monthly pressure
   R2             0.488   n=139

==============================================================================
WHAT TO CONCLUDE
==============================================================================
   The point estimate is stable and the interval is not. That was true
   of the old estimate too, and re-timing the premium has not made the
   threshold precise - it has made the point estimate trustworthy while
   leaving the interval wide, because 139 monthly observations with a
   handful of episodes cannot pin a kink tightly however clean the x.

   Quote the threshold as a point estimate with its interval attached,
   never as a narrow band. A figure that shades a no-trade region should
   show the interval, not a hairline.
