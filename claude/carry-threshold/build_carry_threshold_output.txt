STEP 1  Carry cost, over the interval the quoted spread spans
==============================================================================
   The quoted spread b is the active contract's price over London spot,
   so it spans that contract's horizon. The carry rate from the fitted
   slope, compounded over the same horizon, is what the spread should be
   if New York and London were the same place:

       K(tau) = S * (exp(carry_rate * tau/365) - 1)

   horizon      : median 46 days (range 7-135)
   carry cost   : mean $  8.75/oz, max $ 65.48
   90% band     : median $0.35 wide, 90th percentile $1.87, max $7.67
   carry rate   : 2.78% a year, band +/-0.057 pp
   quoted spread: mean $  8.50/oz
   excess       : mean $ -0.25/oz

   Carry is most of the quoted spread. At 2026 prices and rates it runs
   to tens of dollars an ounce, which is why the raw number is useless
   as a signal and why subtracting it is the whole exercise.

   The band is not decoration. It comes from the slope's own standard
   error in the daily fit, widened by the t quantile because six
   contracts leave four degrees of freedom, and it moves with three
   things: how well the curve fitted that day, how far the active
   contract is from delivery, and the gold price. By year:
      2015: gold $1,160   carry $  0.71   90% [  0.64,   0.77]   width $0.14
      2020: gold $1,784   carry $  4.65   90% [  3.98,   5.33]   width $1.35
      2025: gold $3,434   carry $ 21.28   90% [ 20.45,  22.12]   width $1.68
      2026: gold $4,571   carry $ 24.96   90% [ 24.30,  25.61]   width $1.32


   THE ALL-IN HURDLE. Carry is not the whole cost of the trade. The
   metal also has to be flown and recast, so the spread has to clear

       carry(tau)  +  kappa        the westward trigger

   Shipping is a LEVEL, not a rate. It is paid once, so it is added
   once rather than entering the slope: a cost inside the slope would
   make the hurdle grow with the horizon, and freight does not care
   when the contract expires.

   What that decomposition has done over eleven years:
      year      gold    carry   ship   hurdle  ship share
      2015     1,160     0.71   0.78     1.49       57.3%
      2019     1,392     4.40   0.78     5.18       19.3%
      2022     1,800     9.01   0.78     9.79       13.7%
      2025     3,434    21.28   0.78    22.06        5.1%
      2026     4,571    24.96   0.78    25.74        4.8%


   THE COMPOSITE, AND WHERE ITS ERROR BAR COMES FROM. The two pieces
   are estimated independently - carry from the day's curve fit,
   shipping from a bootstrap over months of customs data - so their
   variances add:

       hurdle = S*(exp(b*tau) - 1) + kappa
       se     = sqrt( se(carry)^2 + se(kappa)^2 ),  se(kappa) = $0.88

      year     hurdle          90% band  carry % of level  carry % of variance
      2015       1.49      [0.04, 2.93]             42.7%                 0.5%
      2020       5.43      [3.83, 7.04]             77.7%                14.7%
      2025      22.06    [20.42, 23.71]             94.9%                17.7%
      2026      25.74    [24.17, 27.30]             95.2%                12.8%

   That is the useful asymmetry. In 2026 the hurdle is 95% carry
   while its error bar is 87% shipping: the large component is the
   precisely measured one and the small component is the guess.
   Sharpening the hurdle therefore means getting a freight quote, not
   a better curve fit.

   At a fixed ninety-day horizon, which is comparable over time:
      year      $/oz   % of spot    $ per tonne
      2015      2.08      0.180%         67,002
      2020      8.59      0.481%        276,158
      2025     40.25      1.183%      1,293,946
      2026     50.28      1.102%      1,616,588

   Relocating a tonne cost $67,002 in 2015 and $1,616,588 in 2026,
   and as a share of the metal's value it rose from 0.18% to 1.10% - a sixfold
   increase. That is worth being careful about, because it cuts against
   the intuition that a rising gold price makes a fixed physical cost
   matter less. It does, but the physical cost is the small part: the
   hurdle is mostly carry, carry is a rate, and rates went from zero to
   five per cent. The barrier to relocation grew because money got
   expensive, not because freight did.

   Shipping was the majority of the barrier in 2015 and is a twentieth
   of it now: carry went from 43% of the hurdle to 95%. Rates and the
   gold price both rose while freight stayed flat in dollars, so what
   stops metal moving is now almost entirely a financing cost, which
   moves with monetary policy rather than with logistics.

   That width is worth holding against the threshold metal has to
   clear, $0.78 an ounce: at the median the carry band is
   44% of it, and since 2020 it has been wider than
   the threshold itself. The carry cost is precisely estimated as a
   RATE and imprecisely as a DOLLAR AMOUNT, because the horizon and
   the gold price both multiply it.

==============================================================================
STEP 2  Check: the excess should be the premium
==============================================================================
   Two routes to the same object. The excess subtracts a carry cost from
   one contract's spread. The premium is the intercept of the curve fitted
   across all of them, re-timed to the London auction. If the
   decomposition is sound they should agree.

   compared with                                  corr   sd of diff
   the premium before re-timing (same settle)    0.998        $0.78
   the premium re-timed to the auction           0.276       $12.14

   The identity holds exactly: 0.998, agreeing to 78 cents an ounce,
   which is the difference between reading one contract and fitting the
   whole curve. The decomposition is sound.

   The second line is the interesting one, and it is not a failure of
   the identity. The quoted spread is struck at the New York settlement
   and London spot three and a half hours earlier, so the excess carries
   the whole of that timing error:
       sd of the premium itself     $ 3.95/oz
       sd of the timing error       $12.14/oz
       correlation this implies      0.309   observed 0.276

   averaged monthly    corr 0.737   (140 periods)
   averaged quarterly  corr 0.822   (47 periods)

   This is the practical finding of the whole exercise. Run the
   cash-and-carry test on the quoted spread, the textbook way, and the
   noise is $12 an ounce against a shipping cost of $0.78.
   The thing being measured is a small fraction of the error in
   measuring it, so the test cannot be run that way at daily frequency
   at all. It needs the re-timed premium, or monthly averaging, or both.

==============================================================================
STEP 3  The threshold: what it costs to move an ounce
==============================================================================
   kappa = $0.78 an ounce westward, 90% CI [$-0.46, $+2.43]
           $-0.95 an ounce eastward

   Estimated, not assumed: estimate_threshold.py finds the kink in the
   premium-tonnage relationship by profile least squares. Freight and
   recasting quotes are private, so the flows are the only evidence
   available. The one public component is the COMEX delivery-out charge
   of $0.35 an ounce, which is about half of the westward estimate.

   This supersedes the $2.99-$3.77 used in earlier work, which was
   fitted to the premium BEFORE re-timing. Noise in a regressor smears
   a kink and pushes the estimated threshold outward, so the old figure
   was too high. The interval still spans zero either way.

   Note what is NOT added on top. Financing the metal from purchase to
   delivery is already inside the carry cost subtracted in step 1, so
   adding transit financing again would double-count it. What is missing
   is the lease income forgone while metal is in the air, which needs a
   lease rate the project does not have.

   days clearing $0.78: 798 of 2,820 (28.3%)
   days clearing $2.43: 368 of 2,820 (13.0%)

   With the lower threshold the premium clears on nearly a third of days
   rather than a tenth, which is the practical consequence of re-timing:
   the band is narrower and it is crossed more often than earlier work
   suggested. Whether the metal then moves is a separate question, and
   step 4 is where the answer stops being automatic.

STEP 4  Does metal actually move?
==============================================================================
   Swiss customs exports to the United States, tonnes a month. Switzerland
   is where London 400 oz bars are recast into the bars COMEX accepts, so
   this is the physical leg of the westward arbitrage.

   139 months with both a spread and a customs figure

   Months grouped by whether the premium cleared the threshold:
   group                                 n  median t   mean t   max t
   never cleared                        30       1.7      2.9    14.5
   cleared on 1-4 days                  50       2.5      7.3   112.4
   cleared on 5 or more days            59       6.6     23.4   195.4

   correlation, monthly pressure vs tonnes : 0.699
   correlation, mean premium  vs tonnes    : 0.632

   The pressure measure is the daily hinge averaged over the month, not
   the hinge of the monthly average: max(0, x) is convex, so averaging
   first would understate a month with a few days far above the
   threshold and many below it.

   The relationship is not stable across the sample. Splitting the
   months that cleared at the April 2025 tariff exemption:
                              n  median t   median pressure $   t per $
   before Apr 2025           49      11.4                0.84      13.6
   after Apr 2025            10       4.4                2.45       1.8

   The same premium stops producing the same shipments. August 2025 had
   a premium above $10 an ounce on sixteen days and 2.7 tonnes arrived;
   January 2025 had $13.64 and 195 tonnes came. The price signal did not
   weaken, the response to it did.

   This is a finding about the quantity side, not a defect of the
   threshold. The candidate explanation is inventory: COMEX registered
   stock rose sharply through 2025, and an arbitrage can be closed by
   warranting metal already in New York rather than importing any. That
   produces no customs record at all, which is exactly the asymmetry
   CLAUDE.md flags as the project's deepest open problem. Confirming it
   needs the daily warehouse series, which the project does not yet have.
