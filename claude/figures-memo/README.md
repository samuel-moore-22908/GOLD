# figures-memo

A seven-page memo written around four finished figures, plus the script that
checks every number in it.

```
claude/figures-memo/
  gold_figures_memo.tex   the memo
  gold_figures_memo.pdf   built, 7 pages
  figures/                the four figures, as supplied
  stats/memo_stats.py     every quoted number, recomputed from the raw pulls
  stats/memo_stats.txt    its output
```

## Build

```
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode gold_figures_memo.tex
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode gold_figures_memo.tex
```

Two passes, for the endnote numbering. `latexmk` is avoided here for the same
reason as elsewhere in this repo: MiKTeX exits non-zero on an unsupported
Windows build even when the PDF is fine.

## Check the numbers

```
.venv/Scripts/python.exe claude/figures-memo/stats/memo_stats.py \
    > claude/figures-memo/stats/memo_stats.txt
```

Reads `gold_final/data/raw/`, which is gitignored and regenerated by
`gold_final/code/pull_us_customs.py` and `pull_databento.py`. Every figure in
the prose appears somewhere in that output, except three that are labelled in
the memo's own comments and come from elsewhere in the repo:

| Quantity | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| 2,862 days in the curve fit; 851 west, 1,287 east; premium sd 0.499% → 0.235% | `gold_final/data/fig2.log` |
| the \$0.75-an-ounce hurdle and its three components | `gold_final/letter/gold_letter.tex`, endnote |
| the episode's \$243m resource bill, range \$85m–\$520m | same, which builds it from 1,632 tonne-legs |

## The four figures

They arrived rendered; nothing here draws them. Each is 540×324 pt and carries
its own title and subtitle, so the memo's captions number them and the
`\fignote` blocks carry only what the figure does not say itself.

| File | Memo | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `combo.pdf` | fig. 1 | the balance plane, by commodity and by partner, in three phases |
| `demand_combo.pdf` | fig. 2 | monthly flows against demand, imports and exports apart |
| `spread_combo.pdf` | fig. 3 | the 90-day spread against carry and the no-trade band, over net metal |
| `NIPA_ITA.pdf` | fig. 4 | ITA against NIPA goods flows, with the gold-adjusted line |

## Relation to `gold_final/memo/`

`gold_final/memo/gold_memo.tex` covers the same argument in the same order
against the earlier Stata renderings of these four figures. This one is written
against the supplied versions, which differ in ways the prose had to follow:

- **Figure 2 is monthly here, quarterly there.** The headline ratio changes
  with the frequency and not because anything was recomputed: 870 t against
  39 t of demand in 2025Q1 is 22×, and 393 t against 13.0 t in January 2025
  is 30×. Both are in `memo_stats.txt`.
- **Figure 3's upper panel is dollars an ounce**, with carry drawn beside it,
  rather than an implied rate. That makes the level of the series mostly carry,
  which the memo says explicitly rather than leaving a reader to infer.
- **Figure 4's adjusted line is discussed as a level as well as a path.** It
  sits about \$18bn below NIPA imports and \$9bn below NIPA exports on average,
  because removing all of HS 7108 and 7115 takes out slightly more than the
  national accounts net out. The earlier memo claimed the line "lands on" the
  national accounts; the offset is visible in the figure, so this one states it
  and rests the claim on co-movement instead.

Two numbers also differ from older work in this repo and are worth flagging
rather than leaving to be discovered:

- The five destinations take **86.5%** of US gold exports to identified
  countries on HS 7108 + 7115. `fig15_flows_vs_fabrication.do` reports 85.8%
  because it does not filter the partner file by heading.
- The episode's resource bill is **\$243m**, not the \$166m central estimate in
  `claude/excess-trade-cost/cost_ledger.csv`. That ledger is built on the Swiss
  and UK export returns (645 t westbound); \$243m is built on US customs
  (873 t), which is the basis everything here uses.
