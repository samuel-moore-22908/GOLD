How much of the premium is the clock, not the market? The London leg is
struck at 15:00 London and the New York leg about three and a half hours
later, so a move in between lands in the premium. The next day's London
auction has already absorbed that move, which makes the next-day London
return a proxy for it: if the premium is partly the clock, the two should
be positively related, and they should be most related on volatile days.
   corr(premium_t, next-day London return) = 0.394   slope 0.194   R2 0.155
