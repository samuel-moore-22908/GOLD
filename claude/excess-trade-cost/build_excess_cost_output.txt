
==============================================================================
WHAT THE EXCESS GOLD TRADE COST
==============================================================================
   Two ledgers, kept apart: resources consumed, and value transferred.

==============================================================================
STEP 1  How much metal moved that otherwise would not have
==============================================================================
   Two legs, both from the exporters' and importers' own customs
   returns rather than from US figures, which carry no mass:

     westbound   Swiss and UK exports to the United States
     eastbound   Swiss and UK imports from the United States

   Each gets the same counterfactual the tonnage estimate used: a
   straight line fitted to every month from January 2015 to October
   2024, carried forward unchanged. The excess is what came in above
   that line, and shipping cannot be negative, so a month below the
   line is charged nothing rather than credited back.

                            westbound   eastbound   both legs
   episode, t                     645          39         684
   whole window, t                695         434       1,129

   The window runs November 2024 to June 2026.

   The eastbound excess is not a second shipment of different metal.
   It is the same metal going home once the exemption of 11 April
   2025 made the tariff risk go away, and it is charged again because
   a return flight costs what an outbound one costs. A tonne relocated
   and then relocated back is two tonne-legs of freight and two
   recastings: 400 oz bars into kilobars on the way out, kilobars back
   into 400 oz bars on the way home.

==============================================================================
STEP 2  What a leg costs, per ounce
==============================================================================
   None of these is a quoted price. No public series for bullion
   logistics or refining fees exists, and the flow-based threshold in
   claude/carry-threshold could not identify one either: it put the
   westward cost at $0.78 an ounce with a 90% interval of [-0.46,
   +2.43], which contains zero. So the stack below is an assumed
   range with the sensitivity carried through, and the one number in
   it that is public - CME's $0.35 depository handling charge -
   sits inside the handling row as the only available check.

   charged per ounce, flat                            low  central    high
   -----------------------------------------------------------------------
   recasting 400 oz bars to kilobars and 100 oz      0.50     1.25    2.00
   secure air freight, transatlantic                 0.30     0.65    1.00
   handling, assay, vault in and out                 0.20     0.35    0.80
   subtotal, flat                                    1.00     2.25    3.80

   charged on value, so it moved with the gold price     low  central    high
   -----------------------------------------------------------------------
   insurance in transit (0.5 / 1.0 / 2.0 bp)         0.19     0.37    0.75
   forgone lease income in transit                   0.51     2.30    6.14

   ALL IN, mean over the window                      1.70     4.93   10.69

   The two ad valorem lines rose from $1.90 an ounce in November 2024 to $3.04
   by June 2026, purely because gold went from $2,651 to $4,242. Holding the
   unit cost flat in dollars would have understated the later legs.

==============================================================================
STEP 3  The resource bill
==============================================================================
                                                   low    central       high   $ millions
   --------------------------------------------------------------------------
   westbound, the five-month episode              31.6       88.3      186.0
   westbound, whole window                        34.2       95.8      202.1
   eastbound, the metal coming back               24.0       70.1      152.5
   --------------------------------------------------------------------------
   BOTH LEGS                                      58.3      165.9      354.7

   That is $146,917 a tonne at the central assumption, on 1,129 tonne-legs
   worth $118bn as they crossed - so 0.141% of the value moved,
   a range of 0.050% to 0.302%.

   The order of magnitude is the point, not the third decimal. Moving
   gold is cheap relative to what it is worth, which is exactly why a
   tariff threat of ten or thirty percent was able to move hundreds of
   tonnes: the arbitrage only had to clear a few dollars an ounce.

==============================================================================
STEP 4  The cost of switching positions
==============================================================================
   Relocating metal is also a trade: a London unallocated claim is
   sold and a COMEX warrant is bought. The price of that switch is the
   location premium - the New York price over London, net of carry -
   which is the series estimated in claude/spread-series.

   For the arbitrageur this premium is revenue, not cost: it is what
   paid for the freight in step 3. It is a cost to whoever was on the
   other side, typically a dealer short COMEX and long London who had
   to close at a dislocated level. So it is a transfer, and the right
   way to report it is separately from the resource bill, never added
   to it.

   The signs are informative. Westbound in the surge the premium was
   positive, so New York paid up for metal. On the return legs from
   April 2025 the premium is often negative, which is the same trade
   run backwards: London paid up to get the metal home. A negative
   entry is not a refund, it is a transfer in the other direction.

   What this line does NOT contain: the mark-to-market on hedged short
   books that were never closed, and the premium paid on metal that
   did not move at all - which in August 2025, with the duty attached
   to the delivery bar and the escape route shut, is where the whole
   shock went. Both need CFTC position data, which is not in this
   repo, so they are named and not invented.

==============================================================================
THE ANSWER
==============================================================================
   Resources consumed moving the excess metal out and back:
       $166 million central, range $58m to $355m
       on 1,129 tonne-legs worth $118bn as they crossed

   Value transferred switching positions between London and New York:
       $165 million net over the window, $216 million gross of sign

   These do not add. The first is destroyed, the second changes hands.
   Against the $118bn of metal shuttled, the resource bill is
   0.14% - which is the finding, not a rounding
   error. The episode was expensive in what it did to the statistics
   and cheap in what it consumed, and those are different questions.
