EXCESS GOLD TRADE: THE AREA BETWEEN ACTUAL AND A PRE-TREND LINE
==========================================================================
   Gold shipped to the United States from Switzerland and the United
   Kingdom, the two places it comes from when the arbitrage runs. Both
   are the exporters' own customs figures, because US import figures
   carry no mass.

   pre-period   2021-01 to 2024-10, 46 months, mean 11.7 t a month
   fitted line  -0.187 tonnes a month, reaching 6.9 t by January 2025

                     actual   baseline    excess
   episode, t           734         35       699
   episode, $bn        65.5        2.6      62.9
   to date, t           896        110       786
   to date, $bn        81.8        9.4      72.3

   The headline is the whole area after the break: 786 tonnes and $72.3bn
   over 20 months. Seven of them fall below the line and net off -11
   tonnes; counting only the months above it would give 796 t.

   implied price of the excess $2,864 an ounce,
   which is where gold traded over those months - so the tonnage and
   the value agree rather than telling two stories.

   Starting the pre-period elsewhere moves it, but not much over the
   episode:
      from 2021: slope -0.187 t a month, episode excess 699 t
      from 2019: slope -0.220 t a month, episode excess 693 t
      from 2015: slope +0.109 t a month, episode excess 645 t

   For scale, over the 13 months where US trade figures also exist
   (2024-11 to 2025-11): $71bn of excess against a goods
   deficit of $1,267bn, so 5.6% of it, and 2.0% of imports.

   None of it was consumed or bought in any economic sense, and a fifth
   went back out within five months - but it enters the trade balance at
   full value.
