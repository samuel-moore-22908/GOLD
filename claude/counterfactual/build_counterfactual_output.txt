STEP 1  What is being counterfactualled
==============================================================================
   Gold shipped to the United States from the two places it comes from
   when the arbitrage runs: Switzerland, where London bars are recast
   into the sizes COMEX accepts, and the United Kingdom directly.

   Both legs are the exporters' own customs figures. US import figures
   would do for value but carry no mass, so tonnage has to come from
   the other side.

   Switzerland   139 months, 2015-01 to 2026-07
   United Kingdom 138 months, 2015-01 to 2026-06
   break at 2024-11, the US election, when tariff risk became
   priceable; episode ends 2025-03, the last month before
   the April exemption

==============================================================================
STEP 2  The baseline is fragile, and that is the finding
==============================================================================
   A single baseline would hide how much the answer depends on it, so
   sixteen are fitted: two pre-periods, with and without a trend, with
   and without seasonality, in levels and in logs.

   baseline          shape                           excess, full   episode
   2021-24           mean                                     662       675
   2021-24           mean, log                                662       675
   2021-24           trend                                    786       699
   2021-24           trend, log                               747       693
   2021-24           mean + seasonality                       650       667
   2021-24           mean + seasonality, log                  666       676
   2021-24           trend + seasonality                      761       690
   2021-24           trend + seasonality, log                 753       695
   2015-24 ex 2020   mean                                     751       697
   2015-24 ex 2020   mean, log                                751       697
   2015-24 ex 2020   trend                                    628       670
   2015-24 ex 2020   trend, log                               457       639
   2015-24 ex 2020   mean + seasonality                       743       693
   2015-24 ex 2020   mean + seasonality, log                  746       696
   2015-24 ex 2020   trend + seasonality                      617       665
   2015-24 ex 2020   trend + seasonality, log                 457       637

   full window   457 to 786 t, a factor of 1.7
   episode only  637 to 699 t, a spread of 10%

   Over five months any sane baseline predicts a small number against
   an actual one that is very large, so the choice barely matters. Over
   twenty-one months the baseline accumulates and the trend assumption
   starts to drive the answer. Quote the episode; treat the longer
   window as an illustration, not an estimate.

==============================================================================
STEP 3  The back-of-the-envelope number
==============================================================================
   Switzerland      actual    546 t   baseline    64 t   excess    483 t   $ 45.0bn
   United Kingdom   actual    187 t   baseline     3 t   excess    185 t   $ 16.5bn
   Both legs        actual    734 t   baseline    66 t   excess    667 t   $ 61.5bn

   implied price of the excess $2,867 an ounce,
   which is where gold traded over those months - the tonnage and the
   value are telling the same story rather than two different ones.

==============================================================================
STEP 4  What that is next to the trade statistics it lands in
==============================================================================
   US goods imports, Nov 2024 - Mar 2025   $1,451bn
   US goods deficit, same months           $615bn
   excess gold                             $61.5bn

   4.2% of imports and 10.0% of the goods deficit over those five months.

   That is the point of the exercise. None of this metal was consumed,
   imported for use, or in any economic sense bought by America: it was
   moved between vaults because a tariff might otherwise have applied to
   it, and a fifth of it went back out within five months. It still
   enters the trade balance at full value.
